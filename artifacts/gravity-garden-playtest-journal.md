A cartoon exploration of real community places, with authored environmental learning experiments.

Discoveries collected: 3 of 10.

## My discoveries

### The living garden — Gravity Garden

At year five, garden A had food/pollinator indicators 40/14; garden B had 45/45. Both used the same resource allowance and time.

My explanation: The diverse mix supports more pollinators and cover, but uses more care and water. The elder takes time, so I would ask the stewards which plants fit this site.

### The community pantry — Gravity Garden

With the same eight-basket harvest, A offered 4/3/1 to households/shared meal/volunteer day; B offered 3/3/2. Unfilled requests were 1 and 1.

My explanation: Moving a basket helps the volunteer day but leaves a household request unmet. I would ask neighbors about urgency and alternatives before choosing.

### The ideas table — West Oakland Library

Ask Gravity Garden stewards about a diverse planting mix that supports pollinators and neighbors. Our garden comparison showed that the mix needs more care.

My explanation: Neighbors and pollinators could benefit, but suitable plants, water, and willing volunteers are needed. Before acting: Which plants fit this site, and how would neighbors decide what the pantry needs most?

## Real places and possible next steps

### West Oakland Farm Park
2847 Peralta Street — West Oakland
Acta Non Verba describes gardens, growing spaces, picnic areas, play structures, and a compost hub at this community farm park. The city launched its free compost pickup hub here in May 2026.
Explore ANV volunteering: https://anvfarm.org/get-involved/volunteer-with-us/
Source checked Oct 24, 2026: https://anvfarm.org/our-farms/west-oakland-farm-park/

### deFremery Park
1651 Adeline Street — West Oakland
Oakland documents mature trees, green fields, picnic areas, recreation facilities, and community gathering at deFremery Park. The neighborhood also knows it as Little Bobby Hutton Park.
Read park & recreation information: https://www.oaklandca.gov/Community/Parks-Facilities/Recreation-Centers/DeFremery-Recreation-Center
Source checked Oct 24, 2026: https://www.oaklandca.gov/Community/Parks-Facilities/Recreation-Centers/DeFremery-Recreation-Center

### Gravity Garden
2519 7th Avenue — Ivy Hill, Oakland
Oakland Urban Farming Project describes Gravity Garden in Ivy Hill as a place to learn gardening, harvest produce, and contribute to a community pantry. Its work includes protecting native elder trees from invasive cape ivy.
Explore Gravity Garden participation: https://www.oufp.org/
Source checked Oct 25, 2026: https://www.oufp.org/

### West Oakland Library
1801 Adeline Street — West Oakland
The West Oakland Branch offers collections for all ages, computers, and meeting and study rooms. Our field-journal desk is a fictional learning scene, rather than an advertised library program.
Explore the West Oakland Branch: https://oaklandlibrary.org/locations/was/
Source checked Oct 24, 2026: https://oaklandlibrary.org/locations/was/

Schedules, access, age eligibility, and availability must be confirmed with each provider. Source checks are not steward approval or a partnership.

Oakland planning information: https://www.oaklandca.gov/Government/Boards-Commissions/Planning-Commission
Before proposing a real change, identify a willing steward and an appropriate planning process/recipient. Ask about ownership, soil safety, drainage, accessibility, water, costs, and long-term care.

## Evidence and authored assumptions

Rain tests partition 100 illustrative drops into infiltration, runoff, and surface water. Dry-period tests use authored retention and delivery factors. Trees use authored year-zero/year-five growth and care factors. Budget/care/water tokens and all 0–100 indicators are uncalibrated game values.
The garden experiment assigns different food, pollinator, and cover factors to vegetables, native flowers, and native elder. It allows four patches, 8 credits, 5 care, and 6 water tokens. Sharing uses eight fictional baskets against nine requested; it makes no claims about pantry policy, actual harvests, or how many people are fed.
The illustrated layout, characters, experiments, and library desk are fictional. Real place listings remain separate from experimental outcomes.
